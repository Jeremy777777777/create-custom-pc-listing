## Description

**MegaPC Custom Laptop — Created Using Lenovo Yoga 7 2-in-1 14AGP11**

**Flexible work modes**\
The Lenovo Yoga 7 2-in-1 14AGP11 base laptop uses a 360-degree hinge to change between laptop, tablet, tent and stand positions. Touch input supports reviewing documents and sharing presentations directly on the built-in display.

**OLED touchscreen**\
The 14-inch WUXGA OLED panel has 1920 x 1200 native resolution, a 16:10 aspect ratio, 400-nit brightness, 60Hz refresh and 100% DCI-P3 color coverage. Its glossy, anti-fingerprint surface supports ten-point touch.

**Processor and installed configuration**\
AMD Ryzen AI 5 430 has four cores, eight threads, a 2.0GHz base clock and up to 4.5GHz boost. This configuration includes integrated AMD Radeon 840M graphics, fixed 16GB soldered LPDDR5X memory and a 512GB internal M.2 2242 PCIe 4.0 NVMe SSD. No RAM upgrade options are offered in this draft.

**AI hardware**\
The integrated AMD Ryzen AI NPU is rated up to 50 TOPS, and Lenovo identifies the matched platform as a Copilot+ PC. Feature availability depends on software, updates and region; no paid Microsoft 365 Copilot or Office license is promised.

**Connections and communication**\
The chassis provides one USB-A 5Gbps port, two USB-C 10Gbps ports with power delivery and display output, HDMI 2.1, a microSD reader and a 3.5mm audio jack. Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 5.4 support wireless connections, while a 5MP IR camera with privacy shutter, four microphones and four speakers support calls.

**Design and final operating system**\
The Seashell aluminum chassis measures 316 x 228 x 15.4mm. A backlit keyboard, precision touchpad, fingerprint reader and IR facial recognition support everyday input and sign-in. Windows 11 Pro is the seller-confirmed final delivered operating system.

**Warranty**\
MegaPC provides a 6-month warranty plus a 12-month extended warranty.
