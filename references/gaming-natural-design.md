## Composition and copy

The computer is the first thing noticed at thumbnail size. Each supporting image communicates one main benefit, with at most a few necessary supporting facts. Choose strengths from the exact verified configuration, not from generic gaming stereotypes. Preserve the six required categories in enhanced MAIN, but use a quiet ordered grid (C05 or a measured C07 adaptation); no mandatory rainbow coloring, oversized hero, deep 3D card stack, or G04 palette. A character is optional. Retain real-LCD clipping, desktop safe zones, Windows asset requirements, white MAIN exterior and MAIN-STRICT rules. Sticker people belong in approved screen regions or eligible PT scenes, never pasted onto the chassis or MAIN-STRICT.

Use believable materials, normal perspective, clean edges and real contact shadows. Reject plastic-looking hardware, halo cutouts, excessive HDR, floating machines, neon fog, energy portals, sparks, explosions, sci-fi reactor scenes and dramatic artificial rim lights in the natural families. Do not alter fan counts, ports, vents, keyboards, chassis proportions or factory marks to fit the composition.

Write concise original English. Prefer specific hardware plus its supported use over inflated promises: e.g. `Room for your game library` with verified SSD capacity, or `A clear view of the action` with verified display specifications. Avoid `ultimate`, `unleash`, `dominate`, `beast`, `next-level`, `unmatched`, invented FPS/temperature claims and unsupported superlatives. Required seller warranty wording remains unchanged.
