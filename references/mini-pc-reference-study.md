## Design conclusion and limits

The combined design inference is a Mini PC series centered on the compact chassis, finish, purposeful connections and a tidy daily setup. The original [M01/MG01 bright studio, M02/MG02 soft desktop and M03/MG03 restrained technical directions](mini-pc-design-system.md) translate those observations into MegaPC choices. The palette hex values and proposed typography/layout rules are our design decisions, not copied or color-sampled facts. Keep one coherent direction per product and preserve the user's preview judgment.

This study supplies appearance references only. Neither a competitor description nor its image establishes a MegaPC unit's installed RAM/SSD, GPU, wireless, display output, dimensions, quietness, upgrades, mount/pack-out or warranty. Do not copy full images, distinctive graphics, slogans, watermarks, logos or badges. Discovering a public reference is not a commercial-use licence. Use separately authorized exact-product sources and the current official evidence chain for production.
