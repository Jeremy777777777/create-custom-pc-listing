# Candidate review rows — user decision 2026-10-21

2026-10-27 amendment: when an evidenced value lacks a suitable Amazon closed selection, always provide a closest actual allowed choice as an explicitly approximate reference under [amazon-selection-contract.md](amazon-selection-contract.md). Preserve the hardware fact; the schema projection is unresolved and the candidate never enters formal export. This separate schema-reference rule supersedes the no-forced-candidate wording below only for that mismatch case.

Every newly generated Research Review and Listing Draft uses the existing three sheets and unchanged attribute columns, with these row labels: Field / Attribute, Definition, Value, Status, Source, **Value Candidate**, **Value Candidate Source**. This explicit user decision supersedes prior five-row and FCC candidate-in-Value wording. Preserve existing historical workbooks and their hash-bound templates without rewriting them.

The candidate is the best evidence-backed possibility for that exact field/configuration, pending confirmation. It is review information, never a confirmed sold fact or customer copy. Keep final_value null and the honest unresolved status. Do not fill candidate cells for VERIFIED or NOT_APPLICABLE facts. No reliable candidate or genuinely tied alternatives means leave both candidate rows blank and explain the gap/tie in Source/evidence. Do not turn an unrelated maximum, starting weight, generic series option, arbitrary price, quantity or SKU into a most-likely installed value merely to fill a cell.

Record optional fact properties `value_candidate` (one literal text/number/boolean) and `value_candidate_source` (nonblank citation). Both must be present together. Require `evidence.candidate_rationale` explaining the ranking and `evidence.candidate_missing_confirmation` identifying the remaining proof. Projection combines citation, rationale and missing proof in row 7 with a NOT CONFIRMED warning. Preserve alternatives in evidence. Zero and false are valid literal candidates only when evidence supports those meanings; missing is not zero. Formula and nonfinite candidates are invalid.

Legacy FCC `recommended_candidate` records remain readable; the new writer projects their candidate/warning into rows 6/7. Only the legacy checker may reproduce their original five-row projection. Do not maintain two candidate representations on one fact. Candidate metadata never promotes itself to Value; verification requires new evidence, explicit fact reconciliation, removal of candidate metadata and normal applicable gates.

The writer requires the seven-row canonical template. It validates the exact candidate/source pair against the ledger. Check-only validation permits unchanged five-row legacy workbooks, but not new general candidate metadata in a workbook missing rows 6/7. Formal export requires the current layout and all existing resolved-fact/copy gates, and excludes unconfirmed candidates. A structure/projection pass is not factual authentication or publication approval.
