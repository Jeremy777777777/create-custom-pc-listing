# Amazon Custom PC — Compliance Rules & Checklist

Distilled from Amazon Seller University, *"Sell Custom PCs on Amazon"*
(Custom_PC_Instructions_Final_Mar_25.pdf). This is the source of truth for the
Customization Computer Policy. When in doubt, the rule here wins over style.

## Compliance checklist (verify before publishing)

Title & brand
- [ ] Title **starts with the seller's own brand** (MegaPC), not the OEM brand.
- [ ] Title contains the word **"Custom"** or **"Customized"**.
- [ ] Title references the **OEM model name** for customer reference (via
      "Created Using …"), without presenting the OEM as the brand.
- [ ] Title includes key specs: **RAM options** and **storage options**.
- [ ] Title ≤ ~200 characters.

Warranty (the single most-failed item)
- [ ] **Bullet point #1 is the warranty disclosure.**
- [ ] It states whether the **OEM warranty still applies**.
- [ ] It states the **seller warranty** (if any) on upgraded components.
- [ ] Coverage is unambiguous — a customer understands it before buying.

Customization scope
- [ ] **Only RAM (memory) and storage (Hard Disk Size)** are customized.
- [ ] **No software customizations** of any kind (no OS tweaks, no bundled
      software presented as a customization).
- [ ] No customization of any component other than RAM and storage.
- [ ] **All customizations are documented** on the product detail page.

Program & fulfillment
- [ ] Listing is created through the **Amazon Custom program** (Product Details
      tab → "Does your product have customization?" → **Yes**).
- [ ] Fulfillment is **MFN** (FBA is not available for Custom PCs).
- [ ] Each shipped unit will include documentation of the customizations.
- [ ] New ASIN + own UPC (reviews from OEM/old listings cannot transfer).

Amazon market research & originality
- [ ] Research covers the relevant accessible exact-model, OEM, configuration,
      and seller-customized Amazon listings, not only one example page.
- [ ] Any user-provided target listing is used as a benchmark for completeness
      and positioning, not as text or artwork to reproduce.
- [ ] Title, bullets, description, image copy, and A+ content are original to
      MegaPC; no competitor wording, images, or distinctive presentation is
      copied or lightly modified.
- [ ] Amazon listing claims are treated as market references unless separately
      verified by an authoritative product source.
- [ ] OEM or competitor listing conventions do not override the MegaPC
      brand-first title, warranty-first bullet, customization disclosure, or
      MFN requirements.

## Why each rule exists (intent)

The whole policy answers three customer questions up front:

1. **Whose product is this?** → Own-brand requirement. Listing a customized HP
   under "HP" implies HP made/blessed it. It's our build, so it's our brand.
2. **What was changed?** → Customization disclosure + documentation. The buyer
   must know the machine left the OEM line and we altered it.
3. **What about my warranty?** → Warranty bullet #1. Modifying hardware usually
   affects OEM coverage; the buyer must understand coverage before purchase.

Frame every element against these three and compliance is natural rather than a
checklist chore.

## MegaPC competitive-research rule

This section is an internal originality and quality-control rule, not quoted
Amazon policy. It operationalizes the user's requirement to achieve the same
level of listing quality as strong Amazon examples without making the result a
copy.

You may learn from relevant Amazon listings:

- which verified specifications customers expect to see in the title
- the sequence in which competitors explain performance, display, cooling,
  design, warranty, and configuration choices
- recurring customer vocabulary and legitimate search terms
- the coverage and pacing of gallery images and A+ modules
- how RAM and SSD choices are presented to buyers

You must not:

- copy or closely paraphrase a competitor's title, bullets, description, A+
  text, comparison copy, or branded phrases
- download, crop, recolor, trace, or lightly edit a competitor's images
- reproduce a distinctive infographic or A+ layout one-for-one
- use competitor reviews, ratings, badges, or claims as MegaPC evidence
- let an OEM-branded listing determine the brand field or disclosure order for
  a MegaPC-customized product

The intended outcome is comparable clarity, completeness, and conversion
quality with independently written MegaPC content and properly licensed assets.

## Key policy text (verbatim essentials)

**What a custom PC is:** a laptop or desktop altered from the OEM's original
configuration. Must be **new** (never used by an end customer), use **new
components**, and be listed under **your own brand name**. Distinct from
refurbished/renewed.

**Customization Computer Policy — key requirements:**
- Custom PCs may only be sold through the **Amazon Custom program**.
- Sellers may only customize **RAM (memory) and storage** — software
  customizations are prohibited.
- All listings under the **seller's own brand**, not the OEM brand.
- Listings must include **documentation of all customizations performed**.
- Listings must state **warranty status in the first bullet point**.
- Titles must follow the standardized format (below).

Failure to comply → listing deactivation, suspension of selling privileges, and
forfeiture of payments.

**Title format (Amazon's stated pattern):**
```
[Your Brand Name] [Custom/Customized] [OEM Model Name] Laptop/Desktop, [Key Specs]
```
**Amazon's own example** (note it uses "Created Using" to reference the OEM):
> "TechBuild Customized Laptop, Created Using Lenovo ThinkPad E16 Laptop 16",
> Intel Core Ultra 5 225U, 8GB / 16GB / 32GB DDR5 RAM, 256GB / 512GB / 1TB SSD,
> Windows 11"

Required listing elements: brand-first title; the word Custom/Customized; OEM
model for reference; key specs incl. RAM and storage; documentation of all
customizations on the detail page; warranty disclosure in bullet #1.

Prohibited: listing under an OEM brand name; software customizations of any
kind; customizing components other than RAM and storage; omitting customization
disclosures.

**Warranty disclosure — compliant examples from the guide:**
> "WARRANTY: Original manufacturer warranty is voided due to hardware
> customization. [Your Brand Name] provides a 1-year limited warranty on RAM and
> SSD upgrades"
>
> "WARRANTY: [Your Brand Name] provides a 1-year warranty on upgraded
> components. 1-year Original manufacturer warranty remains valid on remaining
> components"

(MegaPC's default policy follows the **second** pattern: OEM warranty remains
valid on factory components, MegaPC warrants the upgraded RAM/SSD.)

## Brand options

Unless you are the OEM, you must list under your **own brand**:
- Register through **Amazon Brand Registry**, OR
- Sell under the **"Generic"** brand following Amazon's Generic Product Policy.

Brand Registry requires at least one photo of the product/packaging with your
brand permanently affixed. (MegaPC is the registered brand in use.)

## Brand performance metrics (enrollment + ongoing)

Professional selling plan required, plus:
- Negative review rate (1–2★ over orders): **< 0.65%**
- Return rate: **< 11.0%**
- Product-quality return rate: **< 3.5%**
- Average product rating: **> 4.0**

New sellers without history may still apply; monitored ongoing.

## Create-a-listing workflow in Seller Central (for the seller)

1. Catalog → **Add Products** → **Blank form**.
2. On the **Product Details** tab, answer **Yes** to "Does your product have
   customization?" — this reveals the **Customization** tab.
3. On the **Customization** tab, name the **Surface** and upload a preview
   image. Most products need just one surface. Use the **Preview** button any
   time to see what customers will see.
4. **Add Customization** → configure the **RAM** and **storage** options.
5. **Preview** for an interactive copy of the listing.
6. **Submit** (bottom-right). Publishing can take up to 24 hours.

## FAQ (from the guide)

- **Option dropdown:** only use it for **Hard Disk Size** when offering both
  **GB and TB** sizes (the field allows one unit at a time and mixing confuses
  customers).
- **Returnable?** Yes — FREE 30-day refund/replacement; standard new-product
  return policy.
- **Other configs?** Currently **RAM Memory and Hard Disk Size only**; more may
  come later.
- **Deals?** Not currently eligible for Custom PCs; coupons/price-discount
  promotions are allowed.
- **Move reviews to new ASINs?** No — reviews cannot transfer between ASINs.
  Custom PCs are distinct items with their own ASIN/UPC.
