# Business / Work hero motif library

Apply [confirmed-catalog-defaults.md](confirmed-catalog-defaults.md) and [image-spec.md](image-spec.md). Business front defaults to approved Windows logo+exact Windows 11 Pro lockup, angle to fixed package; safe-fit swaps allowed, but two modes must differ. Both are fully LCD-contained. PT01 has no Windows overlay; PT03 may have one OS-line lockup. A lockup uses an approved fixed original or reproducible crop retaining mark plus complete wording, never generated/retyped substitutes. No usable authorized identity asset means BLOCKED_BEFORE_PRODUCTION, not bare text fallback.

## Classification and entitlements

Choose Business for evidence-supported productivity/collaboration/management positioning; Gaming for genuine gaming hardware/positioning; Student/General otherwise. Mixed positioning requires an explained dominant audience choice, not an automatic gaming theme.

Office/paid Copilot/app logos are not included by Windows Pro policy. Research exact SKU entitlement/product/version/license term and commercial-use graphic rights before any claim. A Copilot key proves only the key; it does not prove Copilot+ or paid Microsoft 365 Copilot. No default Lifetime Office claim. Generic original abstract workflow visuals need no proprietary software UI. All software graphics remain LCD-contained and never imply a physical box in the shipment.

## Creative directions

### B01 — Office Command Center
Verified office-capable workday theme: navy/white, clean abstract documents/windows and restrained blue depth. Only show branded Office assets/entitlements when independently verified.

### B02 — Copilot Work Canvas
Blue-purple glass layers and original abstract assistant/document relationships. Select only verified AI capability claims; a key is not a paid service entitlement.

### B03 — Executive Productivity Grid
Navy/silver with fine gold or bright-blue accents; ordered modular LCD layout and generous reading hierarchy. No inferred enterprise certification/security promises.

### B04 — Hybrid Meeting Hub
Natural light, soft-blue links and original anonymous meeting scene. No copied conferencing UI or implied subscription; camera/mic/privacy facts require exact-model evidence.

### B05 — Mobile Office Workflow
Light gray/blue technical drafting and abstract task timeline. No docks, external monitors, peripherals or outside-LCD MAIN leader graphics. Actual port details belong in PT08.

### B06 — Student-to-Work Toolkit
Bright blue-green, soft geometry and original notes/calendar abstractions. No unsupported school/course compatibility or bundled software claims.

B07–B16 and matching BG07–BG16 motif descriptions are in [business-laptop-gallery-styles.md](business-laptop-gallery-styles.md). All styles use fixed catalog slot ownership and current style approval; same-product local corrections retain approval.
