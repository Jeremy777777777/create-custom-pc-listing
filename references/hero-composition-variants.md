# Enhanced MAIN composition variants

Policy is [confirmed-catalog-defaults.md](confirmed-catalog-defaults.md); implementation is [image-spec.md](image-spec.md).

- `FRONT_SCREEN_CARD`: accurate sourced front computer, centered and complete, pure white outside the LCD with neutral shadow. Screen scene and information subordinate to product. Gaming front follows the current SCREEN_ONLY accepted-reference hierarchy; fit all products naturally without stretching or mandatory head-breakout margins.
- `THREE_QUARTER_SIDE_CARD`: accurate independently sourced angled computer and separately adapted LCD layout; the historical name does not permit information beside the product. Missing real angle blocks this candidate, not a guessed view.
- PT01 may share accurate angle/family but removes Windows treatment and uses a distinct high-level purchase reason.

Business uses one fixed logo+Windows 11 Pro lockup and one fixed package, default front/angle respectively. Other audiences use package in both. All identity pixels, glow and shadow remain inside LCD. No accessory, external device or decorative outside scene is allowed. Gaming head-only TOP exception and six graphical cards follow [gaming-main-reproducible-workflow.md](gaming-main-reproducible-workflow.md); no other content crosses the LCD.

Record source/hash/rights/model match for both views; product bbox/LCD mask; composition ID; Windows source/crop recipe/rights/mode; protected zones; current-hash overlap, scale, style and visual review. MAIN-STRICT remains separate and enhanced candidates require independently verified Amazon exception before serving as published MAIN.
