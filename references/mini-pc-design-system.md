## Layout and text recipe

- Use an available licensed sans serif with a consistent family and weight hierarchy. Prefer a short benefit headline (usually 3–7 words), one brief supporting line and at most three subordinate facts outside the sole complete configuration page. Necessary interface labels are an exception; they must remain legible rather than squeezed into tiny text.
- Let the product dominate the first reading; subordinate modules may use a simple asymmetric grid, flat matte surfaces and generous spacing. Avoid a dense poster full of tiny specification boxes. No fixed pixel coordinates or mandatory resolution: measure each real canvas under [image-spec.md](image-spec.md).
- Keep authentic marks, exact front/back surfaces, vents, feet, buttons, antenna details and port openings. Do not recolor the chassis to match an accent. No added processor badge on the case, decorative R7/i9 blocks, fictional transparent shell or invented internal arrangement.
- Use one accurate machine in ordinary product compositions. Where PT08 needs front/back views, separate and label them as views of the same unit; do not imply a two-PC bundle. Do not import competitor main-image badges, warranty shields, star ratings, discount graphics or seasonal campaign text.
- A compactness claim needs applicable form-factor evidence; any numeric dimensions/weight need the exact product evidence. New physical dimension labels use inches and retain axis labels and source conversions. No invented hand, phone, soda-can or tower comparison; those also conflict with current accessory boundaries.

## Listing copy and research

Apply [listing-style-guide.md](listing-style-guide.md), exact fixed warranty, five bullets and literal Description HTML. Prefer `MegaPC Custom Mini PC, Created Using [Verified OEM Model], [CPU], [Installed RAM], [Installed Internal SSD], [Useful Verified Connections], Windows 11 Pro`, shortening within the existing title limit. This is a pattern, not a claim-filled template: remove unsupported optional phrases, never invent an OEM model, and keep the true product identity.

After warranty bullet 1, lead with the strongest verified compact-desktop benefit; cover platform/tasks, base memory/storage and useful actual connectivity without repeating every spec. Size alone does not prove portability, silent/fanless operation, low wall-power consumption, 24/7 reliability, universal compatibility, gaming FPS, AI acceleration, security or workstation capability. Processor TDP is not total system power. Competitor maximum capacities, multi-display counts, benchmarks, cooling figures, warranties and app entitlements are not this product's facts.

Use the existing official serial/BOM → exact-model OEM → ERP reconciliation → Amazon/deep-research chain, preserving factory/current/sold/capability scopes. This reference study researched design, not any MegaPC product configuration. Do not add the two reference brands to the serial-lookup transmission allowlist solely because their storefronts were studied.
