# Serial-first research trace and candidate decision — 2026-10-26

Customized listing research additionally follows [RAM replaceability/type and seller options](customization-options-policy.md). Record exact DDR/LPDDR family/generation and replaceability; capacity choices are confirmed seller policy, not a platform-maximum research prerequisite. Retain installed base numeric facts separately.

The [evidence chain](evidence-chain-and-deep-research.md) owns research authority and stopping decisions. This contract records their execution inside each selected fact's existing `evidence.research_trace`; it creates no second ledger or workbook columns. New full research requires one trace per selected field. A bounded revision may retain unchanged legacy fields through the explicit reuse review below. Existing read-only checks retain legacy compatibility and validate any traces supplied. The Python projection/writer APIs remain lower-level helpers; they do not replace the CLI's audit.

Each trace has `version:1`, `field_class` and a field-specific `reason`. Classify by the actual Definition: `TECHNICAL`, `SELLER_INPUT`, `SELLER_POLICY` or `DERIVED_COPY`. Nontechnical fields require `basis` identifying the actual seller source, missing input, policy or verified source-fact IDs. Do not misclassify a technical fact to avoid research. Classification and sources still require substantive review.

Technical traces additionally contain:

- `start`: `SERIAL_LOOKUP`, `NO_LINKED_SERIAL` or `REUSE_VERIFIED`. The latter two require `start_basis`, describing the completed serial search or exact still-applicable previous evidence. An inaccessible serial portal remains a serial-first attempt, with BLOCKED route result.
- `outcome`: `CONFIRMED`, `CANDIDATE`, `NOT_FOUND`, `INCOMPLETE`, `BLOCKED`, `CONFLICT` or `NOT_APPLICABLE`; this describes research, not a new workbook Status.
- `routes`: chronological entries with `route` (`OEM_SERIAL`, `OEM_MODEL`, `ERP`, `AMAZON`, `DEEP`), actual `query`, sanitized `reference`, `reviewed_date` (YYYY-MM-DD), `result` (`REVIEWED`, `NO_MATCH`, `BLOCKED`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, `NOT_STARTED`) and explanatory `detail`. Shared document/query references may be reused across fields; each field retains its applicability and result. Do not repeat searches merely to create rows.
- `exhausted`: true only after all applicable routes and promising leads have dispositions. All five route categories must be represented, with an explicit reason for inapplicability. No NOT_STARTED entry is allowed when exhausted. Keep access blocks explicit; blocked research cannot become NOT_FOUND.
- Exhausted traces require `stop_reason`. Unresolved exhausted traces also require `candidate_decision` (`SELECTED`, `NO_SUPPORT`, `TIED`) and `candidate_reason`. SELECTED requires the existing `value_candidate` + `value_candidate_source` pair (or the FCC-specific `recommended_candidate`) and `evidence.candidate_rationale` + `candidate_missing_confirmation`. A tied result has no forced single candidate; alternatives remain in evidence. NOT_FOUND requires TBD, NO_SUPPORT, no candidate and no blocked route.

An early truthful PARTIAL workbook may use INCOMPLETE with an empty routes list and the actual reason/next action. It cannot label a fact VERIFIED or research exhausted. A serial lookup that confirmed one part does not complete other fields. Never generate synthetic traces for real products.

For a changed sold configuration, `evidence.sold_modification` contains `record_reference`, `reviewed_date`, `unit_linkage`, `before`, `after`, `installation_evidence` and `reconciliation`. These must describe actual target-linked assembly/detection evidence; a nonblank string is not authentication. Preserve FACTORY evidence separately and follow the official-priority reconciliation rule.

`scripts/research_audit.py` checks trace structure and outcome consistency. The `research_export.py --write-workbook` CLI runs it before creating a new file. A valid record does not prove its queries were performed or its sources are authentic. Review citations and target linkage separately. Historical ledgers and snapshots are not automatically rewritten or recertified.

Reuse stable research when exact model/region, configuration, relevant components and source applicability are unchanged. Recheck after changes to those inputs, newly conflicting evidence or a source revision. Refresh inventory/price/current warranty observations when used; a new chat alone does not invalidate stable hardware research. Preserve the earlier evidence and actual dates.

## Bounded revision of an older workbook

Changing one field does not require inventing search histories or re-researching unrelated unchanged fields. Use `research_export.py --write-workbook` with both `--previous-ledger <observed-old-ledger.json>` and `--research-reuse <reuse-review.json>` for a scoped revision. These flags are not available as an unbound skip switch. Preserve the previous coherent workbook/ledger bundle through the existing snapshot/Drive archive procedure before generating its replacement; do not leave an old sibling workbook checked against the new ledger.

The reuse review is supporting evidence in the existing run record/delivery, not a second fact ledger. It contains:

- `productId`, `configurationId`, `previousLedgerSha256` and `baselineReference`: exact identity and SHA256 of downloaded old ledger bytes, with the verified receipt or [observed legacy baseline adoption](drive-release-checks.md#existing-product-without-a-qualifying-old-receipt) reference. Never hash the new ledger and call it the previous baseline.
- `reviewer`, `reviewed_date` (YYYY-MM-DD), `impact_review`: actual review of the requested changes, source validity, model/region/configuration and affected dependencies. Recheck volatile seller observations when used. These are current reuse observations, not invented historical research dates.
- `fields`: one entry per retained selected fact missing a trace, with `factId`, a substantive `basis` for retaining its prior status/evidence, and `dependencyFactIds` naming the facts its value/applicability relies on (an explicit empty list only when it has no such dependencies). Shared source reviews can be referenced rather than repeated. Do not omit a changed dependency to exempt a derived claim.

The CLI checks old-byte hash, identity, the exact missing-fact set, unchanged complete fact records, unchanged selected mappings and unchanged declared dependencies. Changed/new facts and affected derived copy require current traces; a previous trace may be reused only when still applicable. The impact review must identify indirect factual changes that structural equality cannot infer. A changed status, source, scope or candidate is a change even if the displayed value is identical. Existing substantive workbook/FCC/serial/export checks remain in force.

Keep unresolved old facts unresolved and confirmed old facts tied to their applicable evidence. Do not downgrade a verified fact merely to bypass the trace requirement. The coverage summary records `researchTrace.state: LEGACY_TRACE_INCOMPLETE`, missing/checked counts and hash-bound reuse details; it never labels old missing searches complete. Deliver the reuse review alongside that summary. Without an actual scoped previous ledger or a defensible applicability review, research the affected fields normally; unrelated image-only work retains its existing scope.

Every new summary includes `researchTrace` and `researchTraceSchemaVersion: 2`, including a summary-only refresh. Refreshing unchanged workbook/ledger/template/product inputs preserves an existing validated `legacyReuse` record. Changed inputs require explicit `--previous-ledger` and `--research-reuse` together, now also supported without `--write-workbook`; the original baseline and applicability checks still apply. New reuse summaries bind each retained fact, its dependency facts and selected mappings through `retainedInputs`. A changed dependency fails freshness checking even if coverage counts remain identical. Handoff checks recompute current trace state/counts/scope and reject false completion claims or missing traces in versioned summaries. Old unversioned summaries without traces remain historical records; old reuse summaries without retained-input hashes can be preserved only with their existing unchanged input bindings, not presented as newly revalidated baseline evidence. Hashes and recorded coverage never authenticate the original source.
