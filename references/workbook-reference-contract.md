# Browser-checked references and display fields — 2026-10-27

New workbook generation, marketplace export and workbook production delivery require `reference-audit.json` beside the scoped ledger. Read-only historical checks may report `LEGACY_REFERENCE_AUDIT_NOT_ASSESSED`; they never recertify old workbooks. The canonical seven-row template stays unchanged.

Record actual browser observations for every URL in selected Source / Candidate Source and projected FCC references: requested and resolved HTTPS URLs, UTC observation time, method (`BROWSER` or `AUTHENTICATED_BROWSER`), observed title, supporting excerpt, applicability/access limitations and explicit outcome. Outcomes distinguish OPENED, NOT_FOUND, GONE, LOGIN_REQUIRED, ACCESS_BLOCKED and RENDER_UNAVAILABLE. HTTP 403/login/CAPTCHA/blank PDF is not a fabricated citation or proof of a nonexistent product. Never manufacture an observation from a HEAD request, snippet or URL shape. Use an existing authorized login session; ask for user access when necessary, never collect credentials or bypass warnings/CAPTCHAs.

Only OPENED references may remain active in a newly delivered workbook. A working page must also support the exact field/variant/scope; a portal, home page or family option does not establish installed-unit facts. Keep unsupported facts unresolved. Preserve failed URLs and original claims in history/audit; replace active URLs only after opening an applicable replacement. ERP migration follows [ERP lookup](erp-internal-id-lookup.md), not blind host replacement. Keep conflicts and factory/current/sold distinctions.

Audit schemaVersion 1 binds productId, configurationId and SHA256 of the exact ledger bytes. `references` contains the observations above (`url`, `resolvedUrl`, `observedAtUtc`, `method`, `outcome`, `title`, `evidence`, `limitations`, optional observed `httpStatus`). `fieldReviews` covers each selected fact exactly once: factId, canonical factSha256 (sorted compact UTF-8 JSON), primaryUrl, basis and supportScope. A nonweb field has null primaryUrl and a recorded SELLER_POLICY, SELLER_RECORD, DISPOSITION or DERIVED_COPY basis. Do not turn a missing seller input into a guessed technical value.

Source text remains literal explanatory text. Source row 5 and Candidate Source row 7 carry one native Excel hyperlink with a clean opened HTTPS target; the target excludes concatenated notes, credentials, raw serials and signed download links. Additional URLs/notes remain visible in the source text and ledger. Candidate rows remain distinct from formal values. HYPERLINK formulas are unnecessary and rejected by the existing no-formula rules.

For built-in displays, Display Maximum Resolution, Display Resolution Maximum, Resolution and Native Resolution describe the internal panel and must agree in horizontal × vertical pixels. Display Refresh Rate in Hertz is a positive finite number, and the paired resolution unit is pixels. Verified panel fields use SOLD_BASE and `evidence.display_scope: INTERNAL_PANEL`, with actual model/configuration applicability. External HDMI/USB-C output limits never fill these fields; retain those separately as explicitly labelled capability evidence. A laptop with a 1920 × 1200 / 60 Hz panel and 4K60 external output therefore exports 1920 × 1200 and numeric 60 for the panel.

`scripts/workbook_reference_audit.py` checks recorded evidence, hashes, field coverage, URL targets and panel consistency. The writer installs native links and validates saved bytes; export and production gates reject missing/stale/failed audits and malformed links. Automated PASS establishes consistency of supplied evidence, not that a researcher really opened a page or that its claims are true. Browser review and per-field applicability remain required. Tests use conspicuously synthetic observations only, never real research certificates.
